2. Who We Are

Bloodline is an independently developed mobile application. For any privacy-related queries, you can contact us at the email address provided in Section 13 of this policy.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information stored locally on your device

Bloodline stores the following information on your device using AsyncStorage (a local device storage mechanism):

  • Game save data — your character's name, age, gender, country of birth, statistics (happiness, health, intelligence, appearance, fitness), career history, relationships, assets owned, and life event log
  • Game settings and preferences — your chosen display preferences, accessibility settings, and gameplay options
  • Supernatural mode data — species selection, faction alignment, ability progression, and supernatural statistics if you use this game mode
  • In-game financial data — your character's fictional currency, assets, and career earnings within the game world

All of the above is fictional game content about a character you create. It is stored on your personal device and can be deleted at any time through the App's Settings screen or by uninstalling the App. If you do not sign in, this data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server operated by us.

3.2 Account and cloud save data (only if you sign in)

Bloodline offers an optional account so your lives survive reinstalling the game or moving to a new device. You sign in with Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. We never see or handle your password.

If, and only if, you sign in, we store:

  • An anonymous account identifier — a one-way hash derived from the account identifier Apple or Google gives us. It is a random-looking string that lets us recognise your saves on your next sign-in. It cannot be reversed to reveal your Apple or Google account.
  • Your game save slots — the same fictional game content described in Section 3.1, copied to our server so it can be restored.
  • A small amount of save metadata — the character name and age shown in your save list, when each slot was last updated, and its size. This lets the App tell two saves apart without downloading them.

We do not store your email address or your name, even though Apple and Google may provide them to us during sign-in. Our server discards them at the moment of sign-in and never writes them down.

Your device also holds a session token, kept in the device's secure keystore (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android), so you stay signed in. It expires after 90 days.

3.3 Information we do not collect

We want to be explicit about what we do not collect:

  • We do not store your real name
  • We do not store your email address
  • We do not collect your phone number
  • We do not collect your location or GPS data
  • We do not collect your contacts or address book
  • We do not collect your photos, camera, or microphone data
  • We do not collect your browsing history
  • We do not collect your device's unique identifiers (IDFA, GAID) for advertising purposes
  • We do not collect biometric data of any kind
  • We do not collect financial information, credit card details, or payment information
  • We do not require an account to play — signing in is optional

3.4 Automatically collected technical data

If you have enabled analytics in the App settings, or if you are using a version of the App distributed through a third-party platform, limited technical information may be collected automatically. This may include:

  • Device type and operating system version — used to diagnose compatibility issues
  • App version — used to identify which version of the game you are running
  • Crash reports and error logs — anonymous technical data used to identify and fix bugs
  • Session duration and frequency of play — aggregate, anonymised data used to understand how the game is used

This data is collected in aggregated, anonymised form and cannot be used to identify you personally.

3.5 Server logs and abuse prevention

When your device contacts our account server, that request carries your IP address, as every internet request does. We use it transiently to rate-limit abusive traffic, and our hosting provider records it in standard server logs. We do not use IP addresses to build a profile of you and we do not link them to your game content.

4. How We Use Your Information

The limited information we process is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To operate and maintain the App — local save data is used to preserve your game progress
  • To back up and restore your saves — if you sign in, so your progress survives a reinstall or a new device
  • To improve the App — anonymous crash reports and technical logs help us identify and fix bugs
  • To understand usage patterns — aggregate analytics (if enabled) help us understand which features are most used so we can improve them
  • To respond to your requests — if you contact us directly, we use your contact information solely to respond to your query

We do not use your information for:

  • Targeted advertising
  • Sale or transfer to third parties
  • Profiling or automated decision-making
  • Marketing communications without your explicit consent

6. Data Retention

6.1 Local device data

All game save data stored locally on your device is retained until you manually delete it through the Settings screen within the App, you uninstall the App, or you clear the App's local storage through your device's system settings. We have no access to data stored locally on your device.

6.2 Cloud save data

If you sign in, your cloud saves are kept until you delete them. Deleting your account from within the App (see Section 7.2) erases your save slots and their metadata from our server permanently. Because the account identifier is all we hold, deleting the account leaves us with no record of you at all.

6.3 Technical and analytics data

Any anonymised technical or analytics data we receive is retained for a maximum of 24 months, after which it is permanently deleted. Server logs containing IP addresses are retained by our hosting provider under its standard retention period and are not used for any other purpose.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you are located, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

7.1 Right to access

You have the right to request information about what personal data we hold about you. If you have not signed in, we hold nothing about you at all. If you have signed in, we hold your anonymous account identifier and the game saves you chose to back up, all of which are visible to you inside the App.

7.2 Right to deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Local saves — Settings within the App, or uninstall the App
  • Cloud saves and your account — open Link Account from the game menu (or the Account section of Settings) and choose Delete account. This permanently erases your account and every save we hold. Your local saves are kept on your device unless you delete them separately.

7.3 Right to opt out of analytics

You can disable analytics collection at any time in Settings within the App by toggling off the "Analytics" option.

7.4 Do Not Track

The App respects Do Not Track signals. If your device has Do Not Track enabled, we will not collect analytics data.

7.5 GDPR rights (European Economic Area and UK residents)

If you are located in the EEA or the UK, you have the following additional rights: right of access; right to rectification; right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"); right to restriction; right to data portability; right to object; and the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Our lawful basis for processing cloud save data is your consent, given when you choose to sign in, and withdrawn when you sign out or delete your account. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 13.

9. Security

Game data stored locally on your device is protected primarily by your device's own security measures — its screen lock, its operating system security, and app sandboxing.

For cloud saves, requests between the App and our server are encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Sign-in tokens from Apple and Google are cryptographically verified against those providers' published keys before we accept them. Your session token is held in your device's secure keystore rather than ordinary storage. Save files are stored in a private storage bucket that is not publicly readable.

No method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We recommend that you keep your device's operating system updated and use a strong screen lock passcode.
