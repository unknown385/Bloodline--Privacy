Delete your Bloodline account

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your Bloodline account and the save data held with it. Bloodline is published by Vertex Interactive. An account is optional: the game is fully playable without one.

The fastest route is inside the game, and it takes effect immediately. No email, no waiting, no forms.

Delete it from inside the game

  1. Open Bloodline.
  2. From the main menu, tap Link Account. (The same panel also appears under Settings → Link Account while you are in a life.)
  3. Make sure you are signed in, then tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm twice. The deletion happens straight away.

If you cannot open the game

If you have uninstalled Bloodline or cannot reach the menu, email vertexinteractive8@gmail.com from the address attached to the Apple or Google account you signed in with, and ask for your Bloodline account to be deleted. We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Data What happens
Your account identifier (an opaque id derived from your Apple or Google sign-in) Deleted immediately. No copy is retained.
Cloud save data (your saved lives and achievements, backed up when signed in) Deleted immediately. It cannot be recovered afterwards.
Saves stored on your phone Kept, because they are yours and they never left the device. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes them.

We do not store your name or email address, so there is nothing else held against your account to delete. There is no additional retention period and no backup copy that outlives the deletion. Server logs, which contain no save content, are discarded on the usual short hosting cycle.

Deleting your Apple or Google sign-in link

Deleting your Bloodline account removes everything on our side. If you also want to revoke the sign-in permission itself, you can do that in Settings → Apple Account → Sign in with Apple on iOS, or at Google Account → Third-party apps on Android.
